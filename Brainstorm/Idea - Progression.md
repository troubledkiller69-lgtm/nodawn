# Idea - Progression

**The pitch:** levels buy identity and access, never capability. The account grows; the survivor does not. A level 1 with an M1911 and a level 50 with an M1911 are the same fight.

## 1. The one test

**Same gear, same skill, level 1 vs level 50: 50/50.** If the level tips the fight, the system is power creep and the store's "no advantage" promise is theatre.

The test applies to every unlock: HP, stamina, aim sway, carry weight, reload speed, stash size, vendor exclusivity, extract count. If a level changes any of them, it fails.

## 2. Two tracks

| Track | Persists | Holds | Lost on death |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Account renown** | Forever | level, cosmetics, vendor contacts, contract board, cell decor slots | Nothing |
| **Survivor** | Until death? | gear, Dollars carried, injuries, infection, raid history | Gear, carried Dollars, the extract bonus |

The account is the wardrobe and the address book. The survivor is the run.

**Open:** does the survivor die permanently, or wake up in the hub with nothing? The Master Context says death has consequences but does not lock permadeath. If the survivor is permanent, renown is still the only track that grows; the survivor's growth is gear and knowledge, not numbers. If the survivor can die, the new survivor inherits the account renown and starts from the same floor. Either way, **the level is not on the survivor.**

## 3. XP sources

| Source | XP | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Extract with loot | 100 + value tier | The largest single chunk. Awarded at the gate. |
| Contract complete | 50–150 | The content engine; pulls players to a zone. |
| Infected killed | 5 | Diminishing after 20 per raid; not a farm. |
| Player killed | 25 | Not dominant; the game is not a deathmatch. |
| First discovery | 25 | Locations, lore, weapon manuals; once per account. |
| Survival time | 1 / minute, cap 30 | Rewards the quiet raid. |
| Revive a teammate | 40 | If co-op downing exists. |
| Dollars spent / items bought | 0 | Never. |
| Time in hub | 0 | The hub is not a treadmill. |

Actions award XP immediately; the extract bonus is the big one. A death costs the bonus and the loot, not the whole raid's progress. That keeps a bad night from being a zero.

## 4. The curve

- 1–50. Roughly 30–40 hours for an average player: 2–4 levels in a first two-hour session, one level a session late.
- A raid yields 150–300 XP. A contract adds 50–150. The curve is tuned so the pass and the level track finish near the same time in a season.
- **Prestige** after 50: reset to 1 for a badge, a title and a cosmetic. No power. Optional.
- No XP debt, no decay, no daily login streak that punishes absence.

## 5. What levels unlock

| Band | Unlocks | Type |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | First vendor row, first contracts, basic cell | access |
| 5–10 | Daily contract slot, jacket category, first decor slot | access + cosmetic |
| 10–20 | Second vendor, weapon finishes, radio | access + cosmetic |
| 20–30 | Military surplus vendor, cell expansion, emote pack | access + cosmetic |
| 30–40 | Cosmetic sets, dog tag designs, a second contract slot | cosmetic + convenience |
| 40–50 | Prestige badge, title, unique outfit | cosmetic |

**Not on the list:** HP, stamina, carry weight, aim, reload, stash size, weapon damage, extra extracts, vendor exclusivity, loot chance. If it changes a fight or a haul, it is not a level reward.

## 6. Vendor reputation

Separate from renown, per trader:

- Earned by trading and contracts.
- Unlocks **stock variety** (a different gun on the shelf, not a better gun) and a **small discount** (5–10%, capped).
- The same guns can be found in raid at any reputation. Reputation is convenience, not exclusivity.
- Not purchasable with Robux or Dollars. Ever.

## 7. Contracts

- **Daily:** 3, rotate. Fetch, mark, recover, eliminate, extract. 50–100 XP, Dollars.
- **Weekly:** 1–2, larger. 150–300 XP, a fixed cosmetic or a currency bundle. No random rolls.
- **Story:** one-off, hand-authored, unlocks a vendor or a location. No power.
- The contract board is how the level system creates **routes** through the map. It is also how the game concentrates players without forcing PvP.

## 8. Attributes

Phase 1 has six attributes, fixed at creation. **Do not grow them with level.** If they grow, a level 50 has more stamina, steadier aim and more carry weight; the test fails, and any future XP sale becomes pay-to-win.

If growth is wanted later:

- Cap it low (a few points, not a second build).
- Make it a **sidegrade** (faster reload vs. quieter steps), not a straight line.
- Allow a respec.
- Never sell it.

But the clean version is fixed attributes plus gear. The survivor creator's choices define the run; the account renown defines the wardrobe.

### Skills

The Master Context §8 says skills improve through use, with anti-farming and diminishing returns, and persist through death. That is power progression by another name. If a level 50 reloads faster, aims steadier and carries more because they used a gun more, the level 1 vs 50 test fails even though no level is involved.

Three options:

- **Cut stat skills at launch.** Attributes fixed, gear and player skill are the progression. Cleanest.
- **Knowledge skills.** A codex of manuals, locations and vendors. No stat effect; cosmetics for completion.
- **Capped sidegrades.** A small pool of perks with trade-offs, earned by use, capped early, respecable. Risky.

Recommend the first or second. The third is how the no-headstart promise quietly dies.

## 9. Renown and the pass

The seasonal cosmetic pass ([[Idea - Monetisation]]) is fed by renown. Free track and paid track, all cosmetic. No XP boosters, no tier skips for sale. The pass and the level curve should finish together; if the pass finishes first, the last tiers are a grind; if the level finishes first, the pass is a second job.

## 10. Death and renown

- Death loses gear, carried Dollars, and the extract bonus.
- Death does not lose account level, cosmetics, vendor contacts, or the contract board.
- The hardcore loss is **gear**, not identity. That is the retention valve: a player can lose a kit and still come back to a level, a wardrobe and a contact list.
- If the survivor dies permanently, the new survivor starts at the same account renown and the same floor gear (basic clothing and a knife). The level is not a headstart; the gear is the run.

## 11. Three models

**A. Renown only — recommended.**
Levels = cosmetics, access, contracts, cell. Survivor has no levels. Attributes fixed. The level 1 vs 50 test passes by construction.

**B. Specialist — risky.**
Levels give sidegrade perks: faster reload vs. quieter steps, better hipfire vs. faster ADS. Capped, no direct HP or damage. The test can pass if the perks are true sidegrades, but combinations create power and the UI has to explain them. Only if He wants build depth.

**C. Power — rejected.**
Levels give HP, stamina, damage, carry weight or stash. The test fails, the store's neutrality becomes a lie, and any future XP purchase is pay-to-win. Do not.

## 12. Open questions

- Is renown account-wide or per survivor? **Recommend account-wide.**
- Does the survivor level at all? **Recommend no.**
- Max level 50? **Recommend yes.**
- Seasonal renown reset? **Recommend no; seasons add cosmetics, not power.**
- Do attributes grow? **Recommend no; fixed at creation.**
- What does a max-level player have that a new player does not? **A wardrobe, a contact list and a contract board. Not a stat.**
- Are use-based skills in? **The Master Context says yes; the no-headstart model says no. This needs a ruling.**
- What is the shape of the level system He has in mind? **Waiting.**
